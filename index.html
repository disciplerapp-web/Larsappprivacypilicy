Large Gym Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Clearlane Development AB ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile application Large Gym (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Large Gym, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Swedish data protection law.

1. Data Controller

Clearlane Development AB is the company responsible for the Large Gym application.

Company: Clearlane Development AB
Email: discipler.app@gmail.com
Jurisdiction: Sweden

2. Large Gym Does Not Collect Personal Data

Large Gym is designed as a self-contained mobile application. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

Large Gym does not operate a backend, user database, or server that stores information about users.

We do not have access to your individual workout history, progress, preferences, or other information stored and used by the App on your device.

3. Workout Tracking and Progress

Large Gym allows you to follow a predefined workout program and track your own workouts and progress.

Workout information and progress are handled locally by the App for the individual user. This information is not sent to Clearlane Development AB and is not stored on a Large Gym server.

The workout programs and exercise information provided by the App are predefined within the application itself.

4. No User Account or Backend

Large Gym does not provide user accounts, online profiles, social profiles, or cloud-based user storage.

Because there is no backend or user database, we cannot view individual users' workout activity or access their locally stored progress.

5. Notifications

Large Gym may use local device notifications to remind you about scheduled workouts or other activities within the App.

Notifications are handled by your device's operating system. You can allow, restrict, or disable notifications through your device settings.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Large Gym does not use third-party analytics services to track user behavior.

We do not use advertising trackers, behavioral tracking, or profiling technologies within the App.

We do not monitor individual users' workouts or activity.

7. Advertising

Large Gym does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

Your locally stored workout information is not provided to advertisers.

8. Sharing of Information

Large Gym does not collect or transmit your personal information to Clearlane Development AB.

Because the App does not operate a backend or user database, there is no Large Gym user database from which your workout information can be shared with third parties.

9. App Store and Google Play

Large Gym is distributed through platforms such as the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Apple and Google may independently process information in connection with downloading, updating, purchasing, securing, or operating applications on your device. Such processing is governed by the respective platform provider's privacy policies and terms.

10. Data Stored on Your Device

Large Gym may store information locally on your device so that the App can remember your workout progress and preferences.

This information remains under your control on your device. Clearlane Development AB cannot access this locally stored information through a Large Gym backend.

If you delete the App or clear its stored data using the functionality provided by your device, locally stored App information may be deleted.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Under the GDPR, individuals may have rights concerning their personal data, including rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability where applicable.

Since Large Gym does not collect or maintain a user account database and does not have access to your locally stored workout information, we generally do not possess individual workout data that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted by us.

If you have any privacy questions or believe that we may hold personal information about you, you can contact us at:

discipler.app@gmail.com

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY): www.imy.se

12. Children's Privacy

Large Gym does not knowingly collect personal information from children through accounts, user profiles, or a backend system.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of Large Gym or our data practices change, or if applicable legal requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Clearlane Development AB

Jurisdiction: Sweden
