Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Lars Gym mobile application ("Lars Gym", "the App").

Lars Gym is designed to provide users with a structured workout program without requiring an account or sending personal workout information to a server.

1. Data Controller

Lars Gym is operated by:

Clearlane Development AB
Sweden
Email: discipler.app@gmail.com

Clearlane Development AB is the data controller for any personal data that we may process in connection with the App.

2. Lars Gym Does Not Collect Personal Data

Lars Gym does not collect or transmit personal data to Clearlane Development AB.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your home address
  • A password
  • Your personal workout history
  • Your workout progress

Lars Gym does not operate a backend or user database where your personal information or workout information is stored.

3. Workout Tracking and Progress

Lars Gym can keep track of your workouts and progress so that the App can provide you with your workout schedule and progress information.

This information is handled locally on your device. It is not transmitted to Clearlane Development AB and is not stored on a Lars Gym server.

The workout programs and exercise information included in Lars Gym are predefined within the App.

Clearlane Development AB cannot access your individual workout history, progress, preferences, or other information stored locally within the App.

4. No User Account or Backend

Lars Gym does not require or provide:

  • User accounts
  • Online profiles
  • A user database
  • A Lars Gym backend
  • Cloud storage for your workout information
  • Social profiles within the App

The App is designed to operate using information and functionality contained within the App itself and information stored locally on your device.

5. Notifications

Lars Gym may use notifications on your device to remind you about scheduled workouts or other App-related activities.

These notifications are controlled through your device's operating system. You can manage or disable notification permissions through your device settings.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Lars Gym does not use third-party analytics services to track your activity within the App.

The App does not use behavioral tracking, advertising trackers, profiling, or similar technologies to monitor your activity for marketing purposes.

7. Advertising

Lars Gym does not use targeted advertising and does not sell personal information to advertisers.

8. Sharing of Information

Because Lars Gym does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not sell, rent, or share your personal workout information with third parties.

We do not have access to the workout information stored locally on your device.

9. App Store and Google Play

Lars Gym is distributed through third-party platforms such as the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Apple and Google may independently collect and process information in connection with downloading, updating, purchasing, securing, or operating applications on their platforms.

Such processing is governed by the respective privacy policies and terms of Apple and Google, rather than this Privacy Policy.

10. Data Stored on Your Device

Information related to your workout progress may be stored locally on your device so that Lars Gym can remember your progress and provide the appropriate workout experience.

This locally stored information is not accessible to Clearlane Development AB.

If you delete Lars Gym or clear its stored data, locally stored App information may be deleted according to the behavior of your device and operating system.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Swedish data protection law, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Request data portability where applicable

Because Lars Gym does not collect or store your workout data on our servers, we generally do not have access to the locally stored workout information that you may have on your device.

If you have a privacy-related question or request, you can contact us at:

discipler.app@gmail.com

You also have the right to contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY):
https://www.imy.se/

12. Children's Privacy

Lars Gym does not knowingly collect personal information from children through user accounts, profiles, or a backend database.

Since the App does not require an account or transmit personal workout information to us, we do not have access to individual users' workout data.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Lars Gym, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated "Last updated" date.
